**Subtitle** (30 char max - this is 23): `Understand every angle`

**Promotional text** (170 char max, editable without a new build):
> News from real outlets, with the context to understand why a story
> lands differently for different people - and how other outlets
> covered it.

**Keywords** (100 char max, comma-separated, no spaces after commas):
`news,media literacy,bias,perspective,current events,explain,context,politics,rss,no ads`

**Description:**
> Lightbulb aggregates headlines from trusted outlets - BBC, NPR, The
> Guardian, The New York Times, Al Jazeera, and more - and gives you two
> things generic news apps don't:
>
> **Illuminate.** Tap any headline for a short, AI-generated explanation
> of why it matters - optionally shaped by your own age range, political
> leaning, or region, so the context actually fits how you'd read it.
> Every explanation is clearly labeled as AI-generated and separates
> what happened (the facts) from why it matters to you.
>
> **Compare Coverage.** See how other outlets covered the same story
> side by side - not just one outlet's framing.
>
> No accounts. No ads. No tracking SDKs. Your preferences, if you set
> any, stay on your device - they're never required to use the app.
>
> Lightbulb also supports hyperlocal news for several major cities, an
> optional daily audio briefing, and a daily-digest notification you can
> turn on or off anytime.

## Before actually submitting either listing

- Re-check the Support/Privacy Policy URLs resolve (Render's free tier
  spins down when idle - the first request after idle can take 30-60s,
  which is fine for a person clicking a link but worth knowing if an
  automated checker times out faster than that).
- Screenshots: neither store listing can go live without them, and
  nothing here can generate them - they need to come from an actual
  build running on a real device or simulator.
- App Store's age rating questionnaire and Google Play's content rating
  (IARC) questionnaire: given no ads, no UGC (Reddit path is off by
  default), and no mature content, this should land at the lowest rating
  tier on both - but answer the actual questionnaire yourself rather than
  assuming, since a wrong answer here is a common rejection reason.
